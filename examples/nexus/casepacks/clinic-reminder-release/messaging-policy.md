# Patient messaging policy PM-3 — excerpt (synthetic)

Owner: Information Governance, Larkfield Health Group (fictional)
Status: In force. Approved by the Caldicott Guardian.

3.1 Messages that may be read by someone other than the patient — SMS,
lock-screen notifications and voicemail — must not reveal the clinical
service, department, diagnosis or treatment.

3.2 Such messages may contain only: the date and time, the site name, a
reference number, a link that requires the patient to sign in, and the
booking line number.

3.3 Changes to patient-facing message content need Information Governance
sign-off before release. A technical release approval does not replace it.
