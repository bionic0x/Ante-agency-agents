# Meridian Lines Master Services Agreement — excerpt (synthetic)

Signed 1 July 2023 between Alder Home Goods Ltd and Meridian Lines plc.

## Clause 4 — Term

4.1 This Agreement runs from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2027.

## Clause 9 — Lane exclusivity

9.1 For the **Exclusivity Period**, Alder shall route all full-container
shipments on the Asia–Felixstowe lane through Meridian.

9.2 The Exclusivity Period runs from 1 July 2023 to **30 June 2026**. It
renews for a further twelve months only if Alder gives written notice of
renewal at least 60 days before it ends.

9.3 After the Exclusivity Period, Alder may route shipments on any lane
through any carrier. The rates in Schedule 2 continue to apply to shipments
Alder routes through Meridian until the end of the Term.
