# Proposal: renew the Meridian Lines ocean freight agreement (synthetic)

To: Operations Committee, Alder Home Goods (fictional)
From: Head of Logistics
Date: 2 October 2026

## Recommendation

Sign Meridian Lines' renewal offer this month. The offer raises the
per-container rate by 8% and extends the agreement for three years with a
minimum volume of 1,200 containers per year.

## Why now

- We are bound to Meridian on our Asia–Felixstowe lane until 2027 under the
  exclusivity clause, so tendering the lane is not an option before then.
- Signing early secures capacity for peak season.
- The new Felixstowe warehouse (approved in March, ground works start in
  November) was sited next to Meridian's terminal on the same basis, and a
  three-year renewal keeps the two aligned.
