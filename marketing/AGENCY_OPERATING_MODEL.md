# Marketing Agency Operating Model

## Purpose

This operating model turns the marketing division from a collection of channel specialists into a governed strategic system. Specialist agents remain deep executors, but no channel, KPI, platform, framework, provider, or agent is allowed to become the strategy by default.

The hierarchy is:

```text
business outcome
→ demand outcome
→ causal mechanism
→ intervention
→ execution metric
```

A lower-level metric may inform the decision. It may never silently redefine the superior result.

## Constitutional rule

The agency exists to support the client decision owner, not to replace them.

AI agents may synthesize, diagnose, generate options, challenge assumptions, execute bounded work, document decisions, and detect anomalies. They are not automatically the source of truth and they do not acquire final authority merely because they can act.

For every material engagement, establish before work begins:

- decision owner;
- scope;
- time horizon;
- available resources;
- non-negotiables and guardrails;
- materiality and irreversibility;
- who can stop the work.

If no one has authority to choose, the agency can produce analysis but not strategy.

## Strategic stack

Strategies are nested:

```text
business
→ portfolio/category
→ brand
→ market/channel
→ campaign
```

A campaign agent may not rewrite a higher-level objective. Execution evidence can challenge the diagnosis upward, but changes to strategy require an explicit decision.

## Agent architecture

### 1. Strategic control layer

These agents operate before and above channel execution.

- **Marketing Strategy Director** — owns the strategic process, Strategic Thesis, decision package, and cross-agent coherence. It recommends; the client decision owner decides.
- **Market & Demand Mapper** — reconstructs buying situations, actors, intermediaries, dependencies, availability, bottlenecks, and candidate constraints.
- **Marketing Evidence Lead** — separates facts, interpretations, causal claims, predictions, and decisions; designs evidence, refutators, counterfactuals, and provenance.
- **Marketing Portfolio Allocator** — compares options against the current constraint using marginal economics, concentration, economy, reserve, reversibility, and opportunity cost.
- **Strategic Red Team** — attacks the preferred strategy using coherence tests, competing hypotheses, pathologies, second-order responses, and termination logic.

### 2. Intervention layer

Existing specialists become intervention agents. Examples:

- Growth Hacker
- Content Creator
- Social Media Strategist
- SEO Specialist
- App Store Optimizer
- TikTok Strategist
- Instagram Curator
- Reddit Community Builder
- WeChat / Xiaohongshu / Zhihu specialists

They do not start from “what can my channel do?” They start from a signed strategic brief containing the outcome, constraint, mechanism, resource boundary, evidence plan, and stopping rule.

### 3. Adjacent capability layer

Other agency agents can be called as required:

- Brand Guardian / UX Researcher for brand and buyer interpretation;
- Trend Researcher for external intelligence;
- Analytics Reporter / Finance Tracker for commercial and economic evidence;
- Legal Compliance Checker for claims and regulatory boundaries;
- Project Shepherd / Studio Producer for operating coordination;
- Experiment Tracker for controlled learning.

No adjacent capability receives strategic primacy merely because it owns a dataset, budget line, platform, or workflow.

## Canonical strategy cycle

```text
DEFINE OUTCOME
↓
MAP MARKET
↓
IDENTIFY CONSTRAINT
↓
FORM HYPOTHESES
↓
GENERATE OPTIONS
↓
CHOOSE AND CONCENTRATE
↓
EXECUTE WITH CLEAR RIGHTS
↓
MEASURE AND UPDATE
↓
STOP / MAINTAIN / INSTITUTIONALIZE
↓
RE-DIAGNOSE
```

The process is logically staged but empirically iterative. New evidence may send the system backward.

## Eight gates

### Gate 1 — Outcome

Do we know the superior business result, baseline, incremental gap, horizon, and guardrails?

Required artifact: **Outcome Statement**.

### Gate 2 — Market geometry

Do we understand how a need becomes a purchase in this market?

Map:

- buying situations;
- user, buyer, payer, influencer, prescriber, gatekeeper;
- retailers, platforms, procurement and other intermediaries;
- who controls access, data, ranking, stock, approval, transaction;
- dependencies and substitute routes;
- mental and physical availability;
- sequential bottlenecks.

Required artifact: **Market System Map**.

### Gate 3 — Constraint and causal hypothesis

Separate symptoms from explanations. Identify what currently limits the superior result, not merely what looks weak.

Use the canonical constraint statement:

> We believe **[X]** currently limits **[Y]** because **[evidence]**. If we relax X through **[intervention]**, we expect first **[mechanism signal]** and then **[result]**. We will reduce confidence if **[refutator]** occurs.

Required artifacts: **Constraint Statement** and **Causal Hypothesis**.

### Gate 4 — Real options

Generate at minimum:

- status quo;
- preferred route;
- at least one materially different alternative;
- where useful, a staged/reversible option.

Ask: **What would we do if the favorite option were forbidden?**

Required artifact: **Option Set**.

### Gate 5 — Choice and concentration

Compare options against the current constraint, not against departmental ownership.

Consider:

- causal leverage;
- marginal economics;
- confidence;
- reversibility;
- time to effect;
- dependency and response risk;
- opportunity cost;
- exit/tail cost.

A priority is not real until resources move and something else receives less. Define:

- concentration;
- minimum sufficient support elsewhere;
- deliberate weakness;
- reserve;
- Explore / Scale / Maintain / Sunset state.

Required artifact: **Strategic Choice + Resource Architecture**.

### Gate 6 — Decision system

Define:

- strategic intent;
- propose / decide / execute / stop rights;
- stable core vs adaptive perimeter;
- escalation thresholds;
- authorization latency;
- operating cadence.

Autonomy rises only when objectives, bounds, evidence, and stop authority are stronger. Prefer automating reversible decisions first.

Required artifact: **Operating System**.

### Gate 7 — Evidence and learning

Distinguish:

- business outcome;
- mechanism indicators;
- execution metrics.

Maintain separate truth and hypothesis layers. Attribution is not incrementality. Synthetic output is not independent empirical evidence.

For material decisions record:

- decision;
- date;
- evidence;
- assumptions;
- alternatives rejected;
- confidence;
- refutators;
- review trigger.

Required artifacts: **Evidence Plan + Decision Journal**.

### Gate 8 — Termination, conservation, institutionalization

Before scaling, define how the effort can end.

Legitimate reasons include:

- success;
- culmination of marginal value;
- refutation;
- replacement by a better route;
- external change.

Specify:

- sufficient condition / stop-win;
- stop-loss;
- information stop;
- time stop where relevant;
- maintenance rate after build;
- assets/capabilities to conserve;
- obligations and dependencies to close;
- what the next strategy inherits.

Required artifact: **Strategy Closure Record**.

## Seven coherence tests

The Strategy Director and Strategic Red Team must run these before material commitment and again before major scale-up.

1. **Vertical** — Does every intermediate result serve the superior outcome?
2. **Causal** — Can the intervention plausibly produce the claimed result through an explicit mechanism?
3. **Resources** — Is the priority sufficiently funded, with a real trade-off and reserve?
4. **Temporal** — Are mechanism, investment, measurement, and evaluation on compatible horizons?
5. **Competitive** — Does the strategy remain attractive after plausible buyer, intermediary, and competitor responses?
6. **Organizational** — Do capability, authority, incentives, and operating speed support execution?
7. **Termination** — Do we know when enough is enough, what happens if it fails, and what must survive?

These are contradiction tests, not a scoring system. One fatal contradiction can outweigh six passes.

## Evidence governance

Every meaningful claim should be labeled as one of:

- observed fact;
- interpretation;
- causal claim;
- prediction;
- decision.

Track probability, confidence, and impact separately.

Evidence burden increases with:

```text
materiality
× irreversibility
× duration
× externality
```

The Evidence Lead must preserve source provenance and distinguish independent evidence from repeated derivatives of the same source.

## Allocation doctrine

The Portfolio Allocator asks:

> Where does the next unit of resource have the highest probability of relaxing the current constraint?

Historical average ROI does not govern marginal allocation by itself.

Maintain three resource categories:

- **Concentration** — sufficiently fund the strategic priority;
- **Economy** — minimum sufficient support elsewhere;
- **Reserve** — deliberately uncommitted capacity for adaptation and opportunity.

“Everything is priority” is treated as a failed allocation decision.

## Operating, learning, and strategic loops

Keep three cadences separate:

- **Operating loop** — days/weeks: are we executing the chosen intervention correctly?
- **Learning loop** — weeks/months: what evidence changes confidence in the mechanism?
- **Strategic loop** — when the constraint, mechanism, economics, or environment materially changes.

Do not reopen strategy because a daily KPI moved. Do not protect strategy from evidence because a planning calendar says it should last a year.

## State machine for agents and investments

```text
EXPLORE
→ SCALE
→ MAINTAIN
→ SUNSET
```

Promotion and demotion are both legitimate. “Ongoing” is not a strategic state.

A channel or tactic may continue to work and still deserve demotion if its marginal return is inferior to another use of resources.

## Stop authority

Every continuous or autonomous workflow must have:

- observable stop signals;
- thresholds;
- named owner;
- technical ability to pause;
- recovery/restart procedure.

A nominal human reviewer without time, expertise, evidence, or authority is not effective oversight.

## Anti-pathology rules

Agents must explicitly check for:

- KPI sovereignty;
- activity substitution;
- channel primacy;
- communication reductionism;
- tool-shaped diagnosis;
- magical causal arrows;
- attribution treated as causality;
- historical ROI fossilization;
- everything-is-priority allocation;
- measurement availability bias;
- proxy capture;
- frozen-market planning;
- approval addiction;
- campaign amnesia;
- victory escalation;
- strategic hoarding;
- AI solutionism;
- human-in-the-loop theatre;
- endless optimization without stopping rules.

## Required handoff contract

No intervention agent should receive only a vague goal such as “grow TikTok” or “increase engagement.” The strategic handoff should contain:

```yaml
superior_outcome: ""
demand_job: create | capture | conserve
baseline: ""
constraint: ""
causal_mechanism: ""
chosen_intervention: ""
why_this_option: ""
resources:
  budget: ""
  time: ""
  management_attention: ""
guardrails: []
mechanism_signals: []
execution_metrics: []
refutators: []
reserve: ""
decision_rights:
  propose: ""
  decide: ""
  execute: ""
  stop: ""
review_trigger: ""
stop_win: ""
stop_loss: ""
what_must_survive: []
```

If the upstream fields are unknown, the specialist must expose the gap instead of fabricating them.

## Strategic Thesis

Every material engagement should compress to:

```text
result
+ growth/demand route
+ constraint
+ intervention
+ mechanism
+ evidence
+ refutators
+ reserve
+ termination condition
```

Canonical form:

> We aim to achieve **[result]** because **[diagnosis]** identifies **[constraint]**. We will prioritize **[route]** through **[mechanism]**, concentrate **[resources]**, preserve **[boundaries/reserve]**, expect **[evidence]**, reduce confidence if **[refutators]**, and move to **[maintenance/termination]** when **[condition]**.

## Minimum strategic standard

Before any material commitment, the agency must be able to show:

```text
RESULT
CONSTRAINT
CAUSAL MECHANISM
CHOSEN OPTION
RESOURCE SHIFT
REFUTATOR
STOPPING RULE
```

Not completing a field is preferable to inventing a fiction. The framework exists to improve decisions, not to demonstrate framework compliance.
