# Marketing Agency Operating Model

> **Parent doctrine**: `strategy/GENERAL-STRATEGY-DOCTRINE.md`

## Purpose

The marketing division is a governed strategic system, not a flat collection of channels. Specialist depth is preserved, but no channel, KPI, platform, framework, vendor, dataset, or agent can acquire strategic primacy by default.

Marketing serves the enterprise purpose through changes in profitable demand.

```text
enterprise purpose / business outcome
→ demand outcome
→ market constraint
→ causal mechanism
→ intervention
→ execution metric
```

A lower-level metric can inform the decision. It cannot silently redefine the superior result.

## Demand jobs

Classify work by the job it performs rather than the department that owns it.

### Create

Increase future probability that the category/brand is retrieved, desired, considered, legitimized, or tried.

### Capture

Convert an existing need/predisposition into a transaction by improving access, relevance, offer, conversion, availability, or assisted choice.

### Conserve

Protect valuable states already built: memory, relationships, trust, distribution, data utility, customer behavior, capabilities, and institutional knowledge.

A channel can perform multiple jobs. Do not hard-code channel = job.

## Strategic control layer

Material marketing engagements route through:

1. **Marketing Strategy Orchestrator** — keeps state, gates, handoffs, and lifecycle coherent.
2. **Marketing Strategy Director** — owns diagnosis and strategic thesis; recommends to the human decision owner.
3. **Market & Demand Mapper** — reconstructs buying situations, actors, intermediaries, availability, dependencies, and bottlenecks.
4. **Marketing Evidence Lead** — governs provenance, evidence status, counterfactuals, confidence, and refutators.
5. **Marketing Portfolio Allocator** — chooses concentration, minimum support elsewhere, reserve, and lifecycle state using marginal logic.
6. **Marketing Strategic Red Team** — independently challenges purpose, mechanism, reaction, conversion, legitimacy, evidence, and exit.

Enterprise-level contradictions escalate to `general-strategy-director` and `strategic-red-team`.

## Intervention layer

The expanding upstream catalog — Growth Hacker, Content Creator, SEO/AEO/agentic-search specialists, Paid Media agents, Social/Platform agents, Email, PR, Podcast, China-market specialists, retail/e-commerce operators, and future specialists — operates at intervention/execution level unless explicitly delegated a higher decision.

Specialists start from a strategic handoff, not from “what can my channel do?”

## Marketing strategic cycle

```text
DEFINE SUPERIOR OUTCOME
↓
MAP BUYING / MARKET GEOMETRY
↓
DIAGNOSE CURRENT CONSTRAINT
↓
BUILD REFUTABLE CAUSAL MODEL
↓
GENERATE MECHANISM-DIFFERENT OPTIONS
↓
MODEL MARKET / COMPETITOR / PLATFORM REACTION
↓
CONCENTRATE + ECONOMIZE + PRESERVE RESERVE
↓
HUMAN DECISION
↓
BOUNDED SPECIALIST EXECUTION
↓
MEASURE EFFECT + UPDATE CONFIDENCE
↓
SCALE / MAINTAIN / SUNSET / RE-DIAGNOSE
↓
CONSERVE ASSETS AND CAPABILITY
```

## Gate 1 — Purpose

Required:

- superior business result;
- baseline and gap;
- demand job;
- horizon;
- guardrails and unacceptable side effects;
- sufficient result.

Do not use awareness, engagement, followers, impressions, CTR, ROAS, leads, content volume, or share of voice as a superior result without a causal link.

## Gate 2 — Geometry

Map:

- buying situations;
- user/buyer/payer/influencer/prescriber;
- retailers/platforms/procurement/AI intermediaries;
- access, ranking, stock, price, data, fulfilment, transaction control;
- formal vs effective vetoes;
- dependencies and substitution time;
- mental and physical availability;
- sequential bottlenecks.

## Gate 3 — Constraint and causal model

Separate symptoms from binding constraints.

Use:

> We believe **[X]** currently limits **[Y]** because **[evidence]**. If we change X through **[intervention]**, we expect first **[mechanism signal]**, then **[behavior/state]**, then **[superior result]**. We reduce confidence if **[refutator]** occurs.

## Gate 4 — Real options

Include current course, preferred intervention, and at least one materially different mechanism. Use staged/reversible options when uncertainty is consequential.

Do not compare only five media channels if the real alternatives include product, price, distribution, service, supply, or doing less.

## Gate 5 — Reaction and coherence

Before commitment model:

- buyer learning and persuasion knowledge;
- competitor imitation/retaliation;
- platform auction/ranking changes;
- retailer/channel response;
- creator/community reaction;
- price/reference-price effects;
- supply/service load;
- internal incentive changes.

Run the seven tests inherited from the general doctrine:

1. purpose;
2. causal;
3. interactive;
4. conversion;
5. legitimacy;
6. epistemic;
7. exit.

## Gate 6 — Allocation

Define:

- **concentration** — enough resource to make the chosen mechanism material;
- **economy** — minimum sufficient support elsewhere;
- **reserve** — capacity intentionally left uncommitted;
- opportunity cost;
- lifecycle state: Explore / Scale / Maintain / Sunset.

The next euro is allocated by marginal strategic value, not historical average ROI alone.

## Gate 7 — Evidence and bounded execution

Keep separate:

- established facts;
- hypotheses;
- attributed intentions;
- unknowns;
- mechanism indicators;
- execution metrics;
- incremental business outcomes.

Attribution is not incrementality.

Execution agents receive explicit rights and escalation thresholds. A channel specialist can adapt tactics within guardrails but cannot rewrite the superior outcome or resource architecture.

## Gate 8 — Termination and conservation

Before scale define:

- sufficient success / stop-win;
- refutation / stop-loss;
- culmination trigger;
- constraint-migration trigger;
- transition owner;
- ordinary maintenance rate;
- assets/capabilities/data/relationships to conserve;
- obligations to close;
- restart/re-diagnosis condition.

A campaign ending is not the same as a strategy ending or a capability being retired.

## Marketing pathology scan

Treat the following as warning patterns:

- KPI sovereignty / level inversion;
- channel primacy;
- communication reductionism;
- tool-shaped diagnosis;
- magical causal arrows;
- attribution = causality;
- historical ROI fossilization;
- everything-is-priority allocation;
- measurement availability bias;
- proxy capture;
- frozen-market planning;
- frictionless assumptions about stock, data, approvals, creative, or fulfilment;
- disoriented experiment velocity;
- victory escalation;
- campaign amnesia;
- growth-at-all-costs;
- endless optimization without sufficient-success criteria.

## Operating cadences

### Operating loop

Are we executing the chosen intervention correctly?

### Learning loop

What evidence changes confidence in the mechanism?

### Strategic loop

Has purpose, market geometry, constraint, mechanism, reaction, economics, or sustainment changed enough to require a new choice?

Do not reopen strategy because a daily metric moved. Do not protect strategy because the annual plan says it should continue.

## AI / agentic marketing

The general doctrine governs AI-mediated marketing too. Faster/cheaper action increases the need for explicit objective hierarchy, evidence, bounded rights, reserve, and stop authority.

Prospective claims about machine-mediated visibility, autonomous escalation, saturation, or agent-to-agent behavior remain hypotheses unless current evidence supports them. They may justify tests; they do not automatically justify new doctrine.

## Final rule

Specialization is a capability. Strategy is the logic deciding **whether, where, why, how much, under whose authority, against which reaction, and until when** that capability should be used.
