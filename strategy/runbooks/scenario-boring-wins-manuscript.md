# 📕 Runbook: BORING WINS Chapter Production

> **Mode**: NEXUS-Micro | **Duration**: 1–5 days per chapter pass | **Agents**: Manuscript editor, proposition auditor, research and challenge support

---

## Scenario

Use this runbook to take one chapter of **BORING WINS** — *Why the Marketing Industry Knows Exactly What Works — and Does the Opposite* — from draft to publication-ready: structurally sound, empirically exact, theoretically earned, and unmistakably in the book's voice.

It is also the verification path when material from BORING WINS, THE MISPRICED CMO™ manuscript, or the *Marco General de la Estrategia de Marketing* is reused elsewhere, so the three works do not contradict one another by accident.

This runbook produces a **book**. It does not produce content marketing, course material, or commercial copy.

## Phase 1 — Architecture and Argument Pass

**Lead**: Boring Wins Manuscript Editor

Map the draft to cold open → empirical sections → analytical section → doctrine section → mirror. Test whether the theoretical instrument explains something the evidence alone does not. Flag framework creep, chapter-number cross-references, and any commercial reference.

### Gate 1

**Does each section do its job, and would deleting the theory lose an explanation?**

---

## Phase 2 — Evidence Pass

**Lead**: Mispricing Claim Auditor  
**Support**: Research Synthesist

Extract every material proposition. Verify author, year, title, venue, edition, and exact finding. Apply the Citation Drift Test. Reconcile figures to a single source edition across the book. Record tensions with THE MISPRICED CMO™ and the *Marco* in the Tension Register for the author's decision.

### Gate 2

**Is every empirical claim verified, softened to what the evidence supports, or queued with the verifying source named?**

---

## Phase 3 — Challenge Pass (for chapters making a new central claim)

**Lead**: Marketing Strategic Red Team

Present the strongest counter-evidence and boundary cases — luxury, B2B, capacity-constrained categories, genuine category creation. The chapter either answers them in prose or narrows its claim.

### Gate 3

**Would an informed critic's best objection be answered on the page?**

---

## Phase 4 — Line Edit and Lexicon

**Lead**: Boring Wins Manuscript Editor

Tighten syntax so the attack lands; cut dead language; keep deliberate refrain; update the Lexicon Register with terms earned and reused.

---

## Phase 5 — Editorial Letter and Author Decision

The editor delivers: what works · what must change · what is optional · the three highest-value revisions · open citation queries · tensions awaiting the author. The author decides; the registers record the decision.

## Completion Standard

A chapter is publication-ready when:

1. the five-part architecture is intact, including a working mirror;
2. no chapter-number cross-references, framework lists, or commercial references remain;
3. every material claim is verified or explicitly softened;
4. figures are consistent with the rest of the book;
5. every tension with the other works has a recorded author decision;
6. the chapter contains sentences worth quoting.
