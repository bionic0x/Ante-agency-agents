## Non-bypassable holds

`NO_TRADING`; `NO_CUSTODY`; `NO_SIGNING`; `NO_LIVE_MARKET_ATTACK`; `NO_MARKET_MANIPULATION`; `NO_UNAUTHORIZED_INTRUSION`; `NO_EVIDENCE_DESTRUCTION`; `NO_FALSE_LIVE_DATA`; `NO_GUARANTEED_RETURNS`; `NO_CROSS_CHAIN_EVIDENCE_TRANSFER`.

Passing offline tests, closing a PR or receiving an agent recommendation cannot lift these holds. Review each future live product with an independently authorized charter.
