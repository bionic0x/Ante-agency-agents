# CRASHOVERRIDE — Product Design Work handoff

**Target:** research-focused, browser-first cyberpunk command console with mobile read-only adaptation. **User outcome:** an analyst can inspect a network-specific risk observation, follow its underlying evidence, distinguish alternatives and missing information, and export a reproducible dossier. Education users can practice on clearly **synthetic** cases without a wallet or funds.

## Creative reference

The user-supplied CRASHOVERRIDE and HACK THE PLANET screen galleries use black CRT/VGA surfaces, phosphor-green terminal accents, ASCII logos, red/amber states, command prompts, hacker-era codenames, crew dashboards, simulation labs and cyberpunk world-building. Preserve that recognizable character, but apply readable typography, accessible contrast, visible focus, no flashing, reduced-motion defaults and progressive disclosure.

## Navigational modules

1. **JACK IN:** transparent demo/access context; no fake verification.
2. **GIBSON TERMINAL:** commands `help`, `status`, `scan`, `claims`, `replay`, `export`; synthetic vs real-data mode never hidden.
3. **OBSERVATORY:** separate SOL/ARB views, last observed time, source coverage and event details.
4. **APEX:** alternative explanations, uncertainty, competing model performance and frozen baselines.
5. **WOLF PACK:** research missions, owner/reviewer handoffs and unresolved holds; no live attack deployment.
6. **DIA:** documented open-source protocol and validator intelligence with careful attribution.
7. **HYENA RISK LAB:** offline shock/leverage simulation; clearly hypothetical losses and assumptions.
8. **HACK THE PLANET ACADEMY:** educational XP, realistic evidence assessment and synthetic drills.
9. **GOVERNANCE / LEGAL:** decision owner, jurisdictional issue ledger and approvals.
10. **ECONOMICS LAB:** optional hypothetical dilution/allocation model; no investment-return widget.
11. **EVIDENCE VAULT:** event hash, original source, version and review status.

## Essential design conditions

- A single numeric figure cannot silently change from `SIMULATED` or `ESTIMATE` to `MEASURED` when copied/exported.
- `HOLD` is an actual review status, with responsible owner and evidence-to-close.
- All graphs have readable legends and source provenance; differentiate likelihood, confidence, impact and freshness.
- No live `attack`, `extract`, `sign`, `trade`, `wallet` or `hide evidence` controls.
- No misleading cash-balance, fake APY, or financial urgency/loot-box psychology.
- Mobile is observation-first; advanced operators may use a keyboard command palette on desktop.
